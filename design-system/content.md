# Content & microcopy

GetSociable is the friend who knows what's on. **Warm, brief, specific.** Never corporate,
never hype.

## Voice

| We are | We aren't |
| --- | --- |
| Specific — "Fri 12 Sep · Ulster Hall" | Vague — "Coming up soon" |
| Brief — "Added to Friday night" | Wordy — "Your event has been successfully added to your plan" |
| Friendly — "Nothing saved yet" | Cute — "Uh oh! Looks like it's lonely in here 😢" |
| Direct — "Leave plan" | Hedging — "Are you sure you might want to leave?" |

## Rules

- **Sentence case** for everything: titles, buttons, tabs, chips. The wordmark is the logo image, not text.
- **Buttons are verbs** saying what happens: "Save", "Add to plan", "Invite friends" — not
  "OK", "Submit", "Yes".
- **Destructive confirmation names the thing:** "Leave Friday night?" · button "Leave plan".
- **Toasts** are past tense, ≤ 6 words, no full stop: "Saved to Belfast weekend".
- **Empty states**: what goes here + how to get it. One sentence + one action.
- **Errors** say what happened and what to do: "Pick a date to continue". No codes, no blame.
- **Dates**: `Fri 12 Sep` in lists, `Friday 12 September` in detail. Times `8pm`, `7:30pm`.
  Ranges `12–14 Sep` with an en dash. Relative ("Tonight", "Tomorrow") only within 2 days.
- **Prices**: `£12`, `£12–£25`, `Free` (never £0). Currency always shown.
- **Numbers**: numerals always ("3 friends going"). `99+` over 99.
- **Names**: Listings, Plans, Hub, Circles, Groups — capitalised as product nouns. An event, a
  plan, a festival — lower case as ordinary nouns.
- **UK English**: colour, organise, favourite, cancelled.
- **No placeholder copy** in anything shown to Peter: no lorem, no "Title here". Use
  realistic UK nightlife data from the existing `DATA` sets.
- **Length stress-test**: every label is tested with the longest real value (e.g. "Belfast
  International Arts Festival") — it must truncate cleanly, not clip.
